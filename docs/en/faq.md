# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is the difference between the iOS app and the web app?

The iOS app is natively developed in SwiftUI and runs directly on iPhone and iPad – no browser required. It is especially optimised for use on stage. The web app runs in the browser on any device, is best suited for text-heavy documentation work, and offers additional management functions such as user management, CSV import, and PDF export.

## Which devices are supported?

The web app runs on Mac, Windows, iPad and iPhone in the browser. The native iOS app supports iPhone and iPad from iOS 26.

## How is LuxStage operated?

LuxStage runs as a hosted service at [luxstage.app](https://luxstage.app). You don't have to run a server yourself.

## Does LuxStage work offline?

Yes, in the iOS app and in the web app. Android follows.

The iOS app stores your shows on the device. Without a connection you can view, create and edit channels, write notes, tick off channels and setup items, edit sections and take photos. Everything is saved on the device, even if you quit the app, and synced automatically once the network is back. The list shows how many changes are still waiting.

If two devices changed the same field differently, LuxStage asks which version should win. Before anything is overwritten, the previous state is saved as a rescue copy that you can restore.

Only available online: editing the structure (creating, changing or deleting towers, battens, locations and slots), creating and archiving shows, and the history. The app then says "No connection".

The web app and the desktop app also work with a local copy. When you sign out, LuxStage warns you if changes have not been synced yet.

## Does LuxStage support multiple users?

Yes, any number – without roles or permission differences. All users can work simultaneously. Changes are synchronised in real time to all connected devices – iOS and web.

## Is LuxStage free?

The iPhone, iPad and Android apps are free. Remote, which controls your ETC console, works without an account.

Shows and GridDeck need a team on [luxstage.app](https://luxstage.app). You try it for one month without payment details. After that it costs €29 per month or €290 per year for the whole team. As a small business under § 19 UStG we charge no VAT, with unlimited users. You pay on the web under Settings → Team, by card or SEPA direct debit. You manage your subscription, invoices and payment method there via “Manage subscription & invoices”.

## What happens after the trial or after cancelling?

Your team stays readable permanently. You can still view and export everything. Nothing is locked or deleted. A subscription lets you edit again.

## Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. The owner opens Settings → Team → “Manage subscription & invoices” and picks the other plan. Time already paid is credited.

## Who manages billing?

The team owner, meaning whoever created the team. The owner can transfer ownership under Settings → Team. Everyone else is a member.

## What data does the iOS app collect?

The iOS app collects **no personal data**. Only the server URL, EOS User ID, and language preference are stored — locally on your device. Show data is synchronised only with your own server. No tracking, no analytics, no advertising.
