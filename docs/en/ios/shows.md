# Shows (iOS)

The **Shows** tab is the home screen of the app. It lists all existing shows as tiles, grouped by venue.

## Shows overview

<img src="/img/ios/shows-uebersicht.png" alt="Shows overview" class="ios-screenshot">

Each tile contains the **show name** and the **date** of the production. Shows without an assigned venue appear below a separator line; shows with a venue are grouped under the respective venue name as a heading (e.g. "Venue 1").

Tapping a tile opens the show directly in the **Setup** tab.

## Create a new show

1. Tap the **+** button in the top right
2. The wizard guides you through template, name and date, active areas, upper machinery and alley towers
3. Tap **Create show** in the summary — the show appears immediately in the list

::: tip
If a venue is selected, the new show automatically inherits the channel structure and drawing of the chosen venue.
:::

## Inside a show

The **⋯ menu** at the top left offers **Close show** and **Switch show**, so you don't end up in the shows list by accident.

After a short break (under four hours, same working day, which changes at 6 am) the app reopens your show on the tab you last used. The next day, or after **Close show**, it starts in **Remote**, where **Continue in …** takes you back to the last show.

The small **Console** button at the bottom right opens **Remote**, **GridDeck** and **Cues** right from the show. The **✕** at the top closes it again.

## Editing

**+** starts a wizard to create a show. Long-press a tile to edit the show details or archive the show. Restore archived shows from the shows list menu or in the web app. See [Editing in the apps](./editing).
