# Settings

**Settings** are accessible via the gear icon (⚙️) in the left sidebar.

## Account

**Change password**

Change the password for your account:

1. Enter **current password**
2. Enter **new password**
3. **Confirm new password**
4. Click **"Change password"**

A new password needs at least **10 characters**. LuxStage does not accept very common passwords such as "password123".

---

**Photos per print page**

Specifies how many photos fit on an A4 page — applies equally to browser printing and PDF export.

- Options: 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12

---

**Sign out**

Clicking **"Sign out"** ends the current session.

---

**Sign out all other devices**

Signs you out on all other phones, tablets and browsers, for example if a device was lost. The device you are using stays signed in. Your password stays the same.

In addition, LuxStage asks for your password again at the latest **180 days** after your last sign-in.

---

**Delete team** (LuxStage Cloud only)

Requests the irreversible deletion of the entire team, including all shows, channels, photos and users:

1. Under **"Delete team"** click **"Delete team"**
2. Enter your **password** to confirm
3. Click **"Request deletion"**

Deletion is not automatic. The LuxStage team will contact you before your team is deleted.

---

## Display

**Language**

Select the display language of the app:

- **German**
- **English**

Click on the desired option – the app switches language immediately.

---

**Navigation**

Choose where the navigation sits:

- **Sidebar** – vertical bar on the left
- **Dock** (default) – fixed bar at the bottom edge; leaves more room for the channel list and more

The choice applies to your account on all devices. The dock is available from tablet width; on smartphones the bottom navigation bar remains. In dock mode the logo appears in the page header.

---

**Unit**

Unit for lengths and heights on bars:

- **m**, **cm**, or **mm**

---

## User Management

**Users**

List of all existing users in your team with email address and a source badge.

**Owner and members**

Every team has an **owner**, usually the person who created the team. Only the owner can:

- invite colleagues and withdraw invitations
- unlock waiting users
- remove users
- delete shows permanently
- manage billing and transfer ownership

Everyone else is a **member**. Members create, edit and archive shows just like the owner. At the top of **Users** you see your role and who the owner is.

The badge shows the user's source:

| Source | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **DB** | Self-created or self-registered – can be deleted |
| **Env** | Configured via environment variable – cannot be deleted |

Only users with source **DB** have a **"Delete"** button.

---

**Storage**

Under **Team** you see how much space your team's photos, drawings and shows use, and how many shows (including the archive) exist. During the trial period you get **1 GB and 20 shows**, afterwards **5 GB and 200 shows**. From 80 % a notice appears.

Once the limit is reached, editing keeps working. From about 110 % you can no longer add new photos, drawing images or shows. To free up space, delete photos or permanently delete shows from the [archive](./archive).

---

**Invite colleagues**

Invites a person to your team by email. There is no password in the email. The person chooses it.

1. Enter **email address**
2. Click **"Send invitation"**
3. The person opens the link in the email and chooses a password. Then they are in the team.

The link is valid for **7 days** and works **once**. Under **"Open invitations"** you see who has not accepted yet. **"Withdraw"** makes the link invalid.

If the address already has an account in the team, LuxStage says so. Without email sending on the server there are no invitations.

---

**Sign-up on the login page**

When **"Allow sign-up"** is on, your team's login page shows a link to sign up. Anyone who signs up there waits until the owner unlocks them under **Users**. The switch is off at first, because inviting by email is the safer way into the team. Only the owner can change it.

---

**Reset password**

Resets a user's password and displays the new temporary password.

1. Enter the user's **email address**
2. Click **"Reset"**
