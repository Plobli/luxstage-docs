# Setup — Locations

**Locations** are freely named setup places, e.g. "3 stands in front of the side-stage door". Each location gets channels assigned. Device, address and color come from the [channel list](./channels); the setup list builds itself. For towers see [Setup — Lighting Towers](./setup-gestelle), for battens [Setup — Fly System](./setup-zugstangen).

## Create a location

1. Open the **Locations** sub-tab in the setup plan
2. Click **+ Location**
3. Enter the name at the top and choose a **type** as a chip (e.g. stand, set piece). Custom types are created on save and are then available to all shows of the tenant.
4. Optionally fill in **place** and **note**
5. Under **Add channels** enter numbers, lists or ranges, e.g. `17, 18` or `1-3`

The dialog checks every channel and offers a decision right below it:

- **Channel missing** – create it in the channel list
- **Channel already assigned** – move it here from its current location

A channel belongs to at most one location.

## Assign channels

- In the **channel rail** click a channel and a card, or drag the channel onto the location. Several channels can be placed into the same location one after another.
- In the **channel list** the **Location** column shows each channel's location. It can be hidden via the column menu. The bulk action **Location** assigns selected channels together.

**Undo** and **Redo** also cover locations.

## Setup notes

Locations appear in the generated setup notes as their own section. In the apps they are a third tab next to fly system and towers; each row can be checked off. In the apps, locations can also be edited under **Areas**: long-press to change name, type, place, note and channels; the pencil mode creates or deletes locations.
