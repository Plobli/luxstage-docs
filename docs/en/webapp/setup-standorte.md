# Setup — Locations

**Locations** are freely named setup places, e.g. "3 stands in front of the side-stage door". Each location gets channels assigned. Device, address and color come from the [channel list](./channels); the setup list builds itself. For towers see [Setup — Lighting Towers](./setup-gestelle), for battens [Setup — Fly System](./setup-zugstangen).

## Create a location

1. Open the **Locations** sub-tab in the setup plan
2. Click **+ Location**
3. Enter the name at the top and choose a **type** as a chip (e.g. stand, set piece). Custom types are created on save and are then available to all shows of the tenant.
4. Optionally fill in **place** and **note**
5. Drop or type channels: there is a single area for channels. Drag channels into it from the side list or type numbers, lists or ranges, e.g. `17, 18` or `1-3`. The input field always sits right below the last channel.

If a channel does not exist yet or already belongs to another location, a hint appears below the field. A channel belongs to at most one location.

## Assign channels

- In the **channel rail** click a channel and a card, or drag the channel onto the location. Several channels can be placed into the same location one after another. Instead of dragging, you can also type the number into the field below the last channel.
- In the **channel list** the **Location** column shows each channel's location. It can be hidden via the column menu. The bulk action **Location** assigns selected channels together.

**Undo** and **Redo** also cover locations.

## Setup notes

Locations appear in the generated setup notes as their own section. In the apps they are a third tab next to fly system and towers; each row can be checked off. In the apps, locations can also be edited under **Areas**: long-press to change name, type, place, note and channels (type numbers directly, e.g. `17, 18` or `1-3`, or pick from the list with the plus button); the pencil mode creates or deletes locations.
